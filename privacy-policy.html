BreakEven – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16/10/2026

BreakEven ("the app") is a trading charges, risk and trade-journal calculator for Android, published by InfraSaathi ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

1. Information you enter

Trades, prices, quantities, notes and settings you enter are stored only on your phone. The app has no account or login, and we do not run a server. We cannot see, collect or access your journal. If you use Android's automatic backup, Google may back up the app's data to your Google account under Google's own terms. You can also export your journal as a CSV file; where that file is saved is your choice.

2. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This happens inside Google's SDK; we do not receive your personal data from it.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some other regions), the app asks for your consent before showing ads and lets you change your choice later under Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads) and opt out of personalised ads there.

See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Charge-rate updates

To keep brokerage-related tax and fee rates current, the app may download a small public file of rates (numbers only) from GitHub Pages. This request contains no information about you or your trades. As with any web request, the host (GitHub) can see your IP address under its own privacy policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, name, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not connect to your broker or trading account, and we do not place trades.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos or precise location.

5. Not financial advice

Charges, break-even and position-size figures are estimates based on published rates that change from time to time. They are not investment advice. Always check your broker's contract note.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Data deletion

Because your data stays on your phone, you can delete it at any time by deleting individual trades in the app, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. We hold no copy that we could delete for you.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
